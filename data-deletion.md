# User Data Deletion Instructions: Van Halen Song Showdown

The "Showdown" app is a private tool for the operator of the Van Halen Song Showdown Facebook Page. It does not
have user accounts and does not store personal Facebook profile data. The only information about other people that it
handles is the public name, Facebook ID and text of comments left on the Page's posts.

To have that information removed:

1. Email vanhalensongshowdown@gmail.com with the subject **"Data deletion request"**.
2. Say which Facebook name you commented under and, if you can, the post and roughly when you commented. (Do not
   send passwords or other sensitive information.)
3. We will delete the stored comments and any record of your name and Facebook ID from the app, and
   reply to confirm, normally within 30 days.

You can also delete your own comment directly on Facebook. That removes it from the Page and from what the app
receives going forward, but a copy the app already saved may remain until it is deleted, so email us if you want that
copy removed too.

For how information is used, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
