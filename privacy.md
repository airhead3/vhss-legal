# Privacy Policy: Van Halen Song Showdown ("Showdown" app)

_Last updated: 

The "Showdown" app is a private tool used by the operator of the **Van Halen Song Showdown** Facebook Page
(the "Page") to publish its posts and to read and reply to comments on them. It is not a public app: no one
other than the Page operator signs in to it, and it is not offered to or used by visitors to the Page.

This policy explains what information the app receives from Facebook and what it does with it.

## Information the app handles

The app uses Facebook's Graph API, with the Page operator's authorization, to access:

- **Page posts and photos**: the text, images, schedule and link of the Page's own posts.
- **Comments and reactions on the Page's posts**: the comment text, the public name and Facebook ID of the commenter
  as Facebook provides them to the Page, the time of the comment, and replies. Visitors who comment on the Page's posts
  are the only people other than the operator whose information the app sees, and only what they have already
  posted publicly on the Page.

The app does not access anyone's personal Facebook profile, friends, messages, or any information beyond the Page's
posts and the comments on them. It does not use cookies or trackers, and it does not show ads.

## How the information is used

- To publish, schedule and edit the Page's posts.
- To show the operator the comments on those posts and to moderate the Page: categorizing comments and identifying
  potentially inappropriate content.

Replies and posts are published only when the operator chooses to send them.

## Where it goes

- **On the operator's computer.** Comment data is stored locally on the operator's computer.
- **Anthropic.** For Page moderation, the text of comments (and any pictures attached to them) is sent to Anthropic's
  Claude API to categorize the comments and to identify potentially inappropriate content. It is used only for that
  purpose.

Information is **not sold**, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and is not used for profiling or
automated decisions about individuals beyond moderating comments on the Page.

## Retention

Comment information is kept until the operator deletes it, and is deleted on request (see below).

## Your choices and deleting your data

If you commented on a Page post and want the app's copy of your comment or name removed, follow the steps in
[User Data Deletion Instructions](data-deletion), or contact us at the address below. You can also delete your own
comment on Facebook at any time, which removes it from the Page (a copy the app already saved may remain until it is
deleted on request).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
